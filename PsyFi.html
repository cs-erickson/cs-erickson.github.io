PsyFi App Prototype

OBJECTIVE:

Develop a prototype app for a customized partient portal for PsyFi TMS, a Behavioral Health Clinic located in Edina, MN. During the project, I took the lead on the majority of the designing and prototyping components.

Phase 1: Research

We began by conducting research: both secondary, web-based competitor research, and primary research interviews with two members of the PsyFi provider team and one of their patients. We also sent out a survey to more of their patients in order to gain a better understanding of user needs. Based on this deta, we determined that the design must:

  • Facilitate communication between patients and providers
  • Allow patients to record and track their mental health progress

Based on the survey results, we developed a User Persona and User Flow Diagram in order to model the average patient's experience with PsyFi:
