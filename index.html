About Me:

Hi, I'm Charlotte!

As a student at Northwestern University studying mechanical engineering, design, and human-computer interaction, I live at the intersection of art and analytics.

I love applying my creativity—but never without a strategic foundation. It’s so satisfying to start from nothing and employ a human-centered design process to create a solution that fits user needs (a fact I first learned through making my own prom dresses in high school). I live for the “Aha!” moments—however small they may be—where everything finally comes together.

When I’m not in class, you can find me promoting inclusivity as part of the Society of Women Engineers, or learning to weld in Baja, the off-road racecar team.
